## 1. Purpose

This addendum outlines California-specific mandated benefits applicable to all health plans operating in California. These requirements are in addition to base plan benefits.

## 2. Mandated Benefits Summary

| Mandate | Coverage Requirement | Effective Date |
|---|---|---|
| Cancer Screening (Breast) | Annual mammogram for women 40+ | 2023-01-01 |
| Cancer Screening (Cervical) | HPV testing every 3 years (age 30+) | 2024-01-01 |
| Cancer Screening (Colon) | Colonoscopy every 10 years (age 50+) | 2022-01-01 |
| Diabetes Management | Insulin pumps and supplies (covered) | 2025-01-01 |
| Mental Health Parity | Equal coverage for mental health services | 2022-01-01 |
| Autism/Developmental | Behavioral health treatment for autism | 2023-07-01 |
| Maternity Care | Minimum 48-hour inpatient stay (vaginal) | 2018-01-01 |
| Maternity Care | Minimum 96-hour inpatient stay (C-section) | 2018-01-01 |
| Reproductive Health | Contraception coverage (all FDA methods) | 2020-01-01 |

## 3. Specific Coverage Requirements

### 3.1 Contraception Coverage

- All FDA-approved contraception methods covered (no copay)
- Any FDA-approved prescription method must be covered
- 12-month supply allowed at one time

### 3.2 Maternity Care (California Knox-Keene Act)

- Minimum 48-hour hospital stay (vaginal delivery)
- Minimum 96-hour hospital stay (C-section delivery)
- Postpartum follow-up visit within 14 days

### 3.3 Mental Health Parity (AB 88)

- No annual or lifetime limits on mental health benefits
- Same cost-sharing as medical/surgical benefits
- Timely access to mental health providers

### 3.4 Autism and Developmental Disabilities (SB 946)

- Behavioral health treatment (ABA therapy) covered
- Up to 24 months of treatment initially
- Medical necessity determination every 6 months

## 4. Penalties for Non-Compliance

- Fines up to $100,000 per violation
- Civil penalties up to $500,000
- Suspension of license to sell in California

## 5. Applicability

- Applies to all claims with region = `'CA'`
- Applies to all plan types in CA (including CA DMHC-regulated plans)
